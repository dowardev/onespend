OneSpend is a free and open-source expense and budget tracker.

Track your daily expenses, organize them by accounts and categories, set
monthly budgets, and follow your finances with charts and statistics.

Highlights:

- Offline-first: all data is stored locally on your device
- Optional end-to-end sync against your own self-hosted server
- Budgets per category with clear monthly summaries
- Recurring expenses and income tracking
- Suppliers and providers management
- Export to PDF and Excel
- Attachments for receipts and documents
- Installable as a PWA, native Android app, or desktop app

OneSpend does not include ads, trackers, or analytics. Your financial data
never leaves your device unless you explicitly configure sync against your
own server.
